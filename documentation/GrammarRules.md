## Grammar Rules
~~~
Every sentence begins with a Japanese honorific relevant to ones rank:
  * Where it differs from Japanese is that Honorifics further have an ending value reflect of the lexical gender of the VOS or OSV that follows: masculine, feminine, and neuter.
Ahuzacos has multidimensional lexical genders: Masculine, Feminine, and Neuter for any adopted language family. Word derived from French use the French lexical gender system, Japanese using pseudo-Japanese lexical gender. Alsatian uses German lexical Gender, and Hybrid uses hybrid specific lexical gender.
  * This reduces down to three lexical genders: Anu Ana, and Anos.
  * This allows for distinct classes in object-orientation that specialize in different language families.
The word order of Ahuzacos is OSV or VOS for the first clause, and SOV for the secondary clause.
  * Object-first can also use OVS for different situations.
Negation Rules: Ne is used for French negation, Ni used for Japanese negation. Nix is used for hybrid negation.
Nouns derived from Allemane mixed with Japanese adjectives will evolve into its own lexical gender system. Eventually this may be its own negation system.
Negated words are front loaded with the negative.
Cursed words are hybrid French and Japanese, specifically French nouns and Japanese adjectives indicating an unusual property or cursed nature.
~~~

## Why Not Italian or Spanish?
This conlang was built with the assumption of French and German lexical gender, which means that in multi-dimensional lexing that reduces down from 12 lexical genders to 8 lexical genders. Or expanding 12 lexical genders to 16 lexical genders. It's already difficult enough to figure out how you'd even use Masculine, Feminine, and Neuter in a multi dimensional manner.
