## Design Philosophy
This offers an example of how I seek to preserve the context around words as much as designing new vocabulary itself.

### Poison Mushroom
Notice that in minetest nothing you eat is really poisonous to eat, unless you modify the game or use Mineclone. In contrast, only Zombie Flesh is poisonous. But in real life you can have poison apples and mushrooms. The further from civilization you get the more likely the mushroom you'll find will be poisonous, same with a random apple you happen to find. How this works out in minecraft is that means that the closer to civilization you get, the more likely you are to get poisoned unless you encounter a witch.

Therefore to modify minetest to have a poison apple or mushroom, you end up with having implications down the road for how the game is played, and therefore what the context around that mod is.

### For Conlanging
The idea of this conlang is to make misusing a term I create so cost prohibitive, that's not its not possible to misuse without also understanding the base language that constitute the bulk of the loan words. So that when you have a poison mushroom or a poison apple, you can be fairly sure they're talking about a random mushroom in the forest, or a poison apple pick pocketing from an adventurer.

Rather than someone referring to someone as being a toxic mushroom, or suggesting their humor burns like a poison apple.

### Implementation Solutions
There are a few solutions for mitigating this problem:

-- Naming elements in the game such that the cost of changing the context around it is impractical.
-- Having NPCs and Enemies speak in the dialogue of said conlang to make changing context cost prohibitive.
-- Implementing the scripting language itself so that its not practical to use the language with that mindset.

This approach does not fix other issues:

-- Dealing with insider threats that know the languages well, and change the context anyway: that's sabotage. [ Harmful ]
-- Well meaning people that happen to know the languages, but inadvertently use emergent context drift. [ Mostly Harmless ] 
-- Other situations I haven't mentioned here.

### Flagging More Advanced Actors
The system of gendered honorifics acts as a tripwire for actors based on the social expectation that one declares their allegiance to people above them. And give instructional language to those of lower honorific station. Which is further split by masculine honorifics, feminine honorifics, and neuter ( like robots and androids ) honorifics.

For example, if the standard word order is: VOS-OSV for those of higher station, and insider threat might use the same word order if trying to get your intention. Or exclude the honorific, rephrasing an instructional as a declaration. This scripting language bakes such compliance and non compliance into the language itself, for a human to detect such activity.
